## Overview

GPT Image 2 Studio provides a prompt-first AI image generation and editing workspace. This refund policy explains how we handle refund requests for paid access, credits, or subscriptions when paid plans are available.

## Eligibility

Refund eligibility depends on the type of purchase, usage status, and applicable law. When paid plans are enabled, refund requests may be reviewed if:

- A duplicate charge was made.
- Access was not delivered after successful payment.
- A technical issue prevented reasonable use of the purchased service.
- A refund is required by consumer protection laws in your jurisdiction.

Refunds are generally not available for consumed credits, completed generations, or usage that has already incurred third-party processing costs, unless required by law.

## How to Request a Refund

Please contact us with the email address used for your account, the order or invoice identifier, and a short description of the issue.

**Email**: [support@gptimage2studio.com](mailto:support@gptimage2studio.com)

We may ask for additional information to verify the transaction and account ownership.

## Processing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method where possible. Processing time depends on the payment provider and your financial institution.

## Changes

We may update this refund policy as our product, pricing, and payment providers evolve. The latest version will be posted on this page.
