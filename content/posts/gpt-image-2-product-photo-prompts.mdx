Product photos have a different job from editorial images. They need to show the object clearly, make the material believable, preserve readable labels, and fit a product page or ad crop. GPT Image 2 is useful here because it can follow detailed image instructions and can also edit from an existing reference image.

This guide gives product photo prompt patterns for ecommerce sellers, Shopify stores, DTC brands, and marketers.

## Product Prompt Formula

Use this structure:

```text
Create a [channel] product photo of [product] on [surface or scene].
Show [materials, shape, label, scale, props].
Lighting: [direction and mood].
Text: label must read "[exact label]".
Style: [commercial, lifestyle, premium, minimal, natural].
Format: [aspect ratio].
Constraints: no extra logos, no misspelled text, no distorted product.
```

The most important phrase is often "label must read." If text accuracy matters, keep the text short and specific.

## Prompt 1: Clean Ecommerce Hero

```text
Create a clean ecommerce hero photo of a premium skincare serum bottle on a white marble surface. Soft daylight from the left, realistic contact shadow, subtle reflection, transparent glass texture. The label must read "HYDRA GLOW SERUM". Minimal luxury background, no extra text, no logo, 4:5 vertical.
```

Use this for product pages, collection headers, and paid ads where the product needs to be the first thing shoppers see.

## Prompt 2: Packaging Mockup

```text
Create a 3D packaging mockup for an organic matcha powder pouch and matching box. Front label text must read "KIZUNA MATCHA". Use a soft cream background, green accent linework, realistic paper texture, subtle shadows, premium Japanese grocery packaging, 16:9.
```

Packaging prompts should include material and print finish. Words like matte paper, foil stamp, clear label, glass, metal, or soft pouch help the image feel more concrete.

## Prompt 3: Lifestyle Product Scene

```text
Create a lifestyle product photo for a compact desk lamp on a modern work desk. Show a laptop, notebook, and warm evening lighting. The product is matte white with a brushed metal neck. Clean creator workspace, realistic shadows, shallow depth of field, no people, 16:9.
```

Lifestyle prompts help buyers imagine use. Keep props relevant and few. Too many props can pull attention away from the item.

## Prompt 4: Social Ad Product Image

```text
Create a vertical social ad for a reusable water bottle. Bottle centered, condensation droplets, summer outdoor color palette, bold headline text "STAY COLD ALL DAY", small CTA text "SHOP NOW". High contrast, clean retail design, readable mobile text, 9:16.
```

Social ad prompts should include the offer or hook directly in the image. If you need more ad examples, continue with the [GPT Image 2 poster prompts guide](/blog/gpt-image-2-poster-prompts).

## Prompt 5: Reference-Based Product Edit

If you have an existing product shot, upload it and edit with a preservation-first prompt:

```text
Keep the product shape, label, color, and camera angle unchanged. Replace the background with a clean light-gray studio set. Add soft shadow under the product, improve exposure, and remove dust. Do not change any label text.
```

This is useful when you already have a real product photo but need a cleaner background or better lighting.

## Quality Checklist

Before using a generated product image, inspect:

- Label spelling and text placement.
- Product shape and proportions.
- Material realism.
- Contact shadow and reflection.
- Crop safety for mobile.
- Background clutter.
- Any fake brand marks or extra symbols.

Product visuals should look boring in the right ways. Shoppers need clarity before novelty.
